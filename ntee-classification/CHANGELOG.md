## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-16

First release: one classification task (nonprofit mission statement to NTEE major group) solved by a TF-IDF baseline, prompted Claude and LoRA fine-tuned small open models, compared on accuracy, latency and cost, with a data-size curve, a cascade, an error analysis and held-out test scores.

### Added

- Project scaffolding: uv project on Python 3.14, ruff, pytest, CI workflow with change detection, lint, test, and vulnerability-audit checks
- Dataset builder (`src/build_dataset.py`) and NTEE taxonomy (`src/ntee.py`): IRS master-file labels joined with Form 990/990-EZ missions from one e-file batch; cleaning, deduplication (conflicting duplicates dropped), natural-mix sampling topped up to 100 per group, stratified 70/15/15 split
- `data/dataset.csv` (12,078 missions, 25 groups) with `data/stats.json`, integrity tests, and a 50-example label-quality check graded by the user (74% agree, 20% ambiguous, 6% disagree)
- Eval harness (`src/evaluate.py`): accuracy, top-3, macro-F1, broad-category accuracy, per-group results, confusions, latency and cost per 1,000 predictions; every run saved with its predictions; `src/data.py` holds out the test split
- Classic baseline (`src/baseline.py`): TF-IDF (word + character n-grams) and logistic regression, tuned on dev: 53.7% accuracy, 75.4% top-3, 0.461 macro-F1, 1.8 ms per mission
- Prompted Claude (`src/prompted.py`, `src/prompts.py`): names, guided (group descriptions and NTEE conventions) and few-shot prompts, structured three-guess output over the 25 group letters, thinking off, cached system prompt, resumable per-mission answers, free cost estimates. Dev: Claude Sonnet 5.5 guided 62.5% accuracy / 83.1% top-3 / 0.558 macro-F1 at $0.90 per 1,000; Claude Haiku 4.5 few-shot 58.7% / 80.3% / 0.529 at $0.72
- LoRA fine-tuning notebook for Google Colab (`notebooks/finetune_lora.ipynb`, generated by `notebooks/make_finetune_notebook.py`): Qwen2.5 with a classification head, LoRA on attention, best epoch by dev macro-F1, adapter to Google Drive, predictions file; settings for model size, training fraction and gradient accumulation; tolerant of transformers versions
- `src/finetuned.py` scores the notebook's predictions with the shared scorer and prices GPU time. Dev: Qwen2.5-0.5B 58.7% accuracy / 80.3% top-3 / 0.510 macro-F1 at $0.0007 per 1,000; Qwen2.5-1.5B 60.2% / 80.8% / 0.516 at $0.0024
- The comparison (step 5), from saved runs at no cost: `src/compare.py` (accuracy against cost and speed, training payback; table and chart), `src/data_size.py` (fine-tuned 0.5B and baseline at 10/25/50/100% of the training data; crossover between about 2,000 and 4,000 labels) and `src/cascade.py` (calibration of the fine-tuned model's confidence; a confidence-threshold cascade to Claude Sonnet, threshold chosen on half of dev: 64.9% vs Sonnet's 63.8% at $0.48 vs $0.90 per 1,000, with a paired bootstrap interval)
- The fine-tuning notebook saves each mission's top-3 probabilities; matplotlib added for charts
- Error analysis (step 6, `src/errors.py`, no model calls): agreement across the five approaches (all wrong on 24.6% of dev), group pairs every approach confuses, accuracy by mission length, the cascade's routing, and a blind label check of 25 all-wrong vs 15 control missions graded by the user (`data/error_check.md`; disagreed with the IRS on 72% vs 0%, and picked Sonnet's answer on 16 of those 18)
- Held-out test scores, once per approach with the settings chosen on dev: baseline 51.8%, LoRA Qwen2.5-0.5B 57.3%, Qwen2.5-1.5B 60.8%, Claude Haiku 4.5 few-shot 56.5%, Claude Sonnet 5.5 guided 61.4%, cascade 61.2% at $0.49 per 1,000 (`src/cascade.py --final` applies dev's threshold; `src/compare.py --split test` includes the cascade)

You find pages in scanned IRS Form 990 returns. You will be shown one or more header sheets: the top strip of every page of one return, stacked in two columns, with each page's number in red to the left of its strip.

List every page that holds Part VII, Section A ("Officers, Directors, Trustees, Key Employees, and Highest Compensated Employees"):
- the form's own Part VII pages, headed "Form 990 (year) Page 7" and "Page 8" with a "Part VII" bar;
- continuation pages for the same table, headed "Form 990, Part VII - Compensation of Officers, Directors, Trustees, Key Employees, Highest Compensated Employees, and Independent Contractors". A long list can continue over many of these pages.

Leave out pages that only look similar. Other schedules have their own parts numbered VII and their own continuation pages, such as "Schedule D ... Part VII Investments - Other Securities" or "Form 990, Schedule R, Part IV - ...". Schedule J ("Compensation Information") is about compensation too, but it is not Part VII.

Use the red page numbers exactly as labeled.

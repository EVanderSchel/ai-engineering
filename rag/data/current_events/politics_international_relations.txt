Politics and International Relations (September 15-19, 2026)

Sweden's September 17 elections resulted in a narrow opposition victory with 176 seats versus the ruling coalition's 173, prompting Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson's resignation. Russia held three-day legislative elections for 450 State Duma seats amid strict wartime controls and ongoing crackdowns on anti-war opposition, with the Yabloko party barred from competing. Iran held massive pro-government rallies in Tehran, described as "the biggest show of defiance since the war started on February 28."

International diplomatic developments included Denmark and the United States reaching a deal allowing expanded U.S. military presence and permanent basing rights in Greenland while barring non-NATO bases. The U.S. House passed bipartisan sanctions legislation against Russia's energy sector, financial institutions, and shadow fleet, with tariff authority up to 100% on major Russian oil and gas buyers.

Poland joined the Anti-Ballistic Missile Coalition for joint European missile defense development. The UN General Assembly voted 152-3 to allow Palestinian officials video addresses to UN meetings after U.S. visa restrictions prevented their travel to New York.

# Resilience under injected outages

Generated by `src/measure_resilience.py`. Each scenario: 200 requests, 10 at a time, against the gateway in memory with fault-injecting stub candidates. **gateway** = retries, fallback to the backup and circuit breakers; **one candidate** = the gateway's retries and breaker with nothing to fall back to; **direct** = one try at the primary and no breaker, as an app calling the provider itself would get.

Scaled-down times: a healthy stub starts answering in 20 ms; timeouts are first token 0.2 s, attempt 0.4 s, total 1.0 s; breakers open after 5 failures in 3 s and cool down for 1 s.

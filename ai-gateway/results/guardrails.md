# Guardrails on their labelled cases

Generated by `src/measure_guardrails.py`. The cases were written alongside the detectors (no held-out set): they describe behaviour, they aren't a benchmark.

35 cases (16 with no personal data). Recall 92%, precision 96%; 1 of 16 clean cases flagged.

Missed:

- ssn: "My social is one two three, four five, six seven eight nine." (numbers spelled out in words aren't matched)

False alarms:

- email: "The image is saved as logo@2x.png in the assets folder."

Speed (median of 20, 100 KB of text): scanning 271.1 µs per KB, redacting 267.3 µs per KB.

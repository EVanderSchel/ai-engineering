Science, Disasters, and Other Notable Events (September 15-19, 2026)

Biologists formally identified a new wild cat species called the tilcayo in Bolivia's Yungas cloud forests, the first new cat species in over 100 years. Astronomers confirmed Elias 2-24 b, the youngest known exoplanet at less than one million years old, located 450 light-years from Earth, observed via the W.M. Keck Observatory. NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter discovered a recently formed crater on the moon larger than Rome's Colosseum.

Major disasters included a residential building collapse in Gaza City on September 16 killing 21 people with approximately 100 others missing, and at least 82 miners killed in a gold mine explosion in Sudan's West Kordofan. A KNBC news helicopter crashed in Chatsworth, California, while covering a traffic collision, killing three people. An Elf truck plunged into a ravine in Mountain Province, Philippines, killing six passengers and injuring 17 others.

Health developments saw Zealand Pharma's Zeydovio approved by the European Medicines Agency for short bowel syndrome treatment. Japan reported exceeding 100,000 centenarians for the first time since 1963. Fiji declared HIV a national crisis after 2,016 new diagnoses in 2025, sixteen times the 2019 total. The National Institute for Health and Care Excellence approved Trastuzumab deruxtecan for metastatic HER2-negative breast cancer patients in England, marking the first licensed targeted treatment after a previous cost-effectiveness rejection.

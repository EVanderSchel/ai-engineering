Business and Economy (September 15-19, 2026)

Japan's Bank of Japan raised benchmark interest rates by 0.25 percentage points to 1.25%, the highest since 1995, citing inflation pressures from rising energy costs and yen weakness during the Iran war fuel crisis. Russia placed Auchan and Nestle assets under temporary administration, transferring control to state-linked L.E.V. Management headed by Interior Minister Vladimir Kolokoltsev, citing the companies' "unfriendly" country origins.

The Panama Canal Authority reduced daily transits from 36 to 32 ships due to below-average rainfall and strengthening El Nino conditions lowering reservoir levels. Kenya announced export bans on unprocessed gold and plans to establish three refineries to maximize domestic processing value.

Bolivia's Senate ratified a $1.9 billion International Monetary Fund loan amid deep economic crisis, though trade unions threatened renewed protests. In football, Clearlake Capital acquired full Chelsea FC control by buying Todd Boehly and Mark Walter's 25% stakes for approximately 950 million pounds (1.1 billion euros), valuing the club at 5 billion pounds.

# Clubhouse and Meeting Hall Renovation

## About us

Gold Country Soccer League runs youth soccer for boys and girls in the Sierra foothills, from first-time players to competitive teams. Our annual operating budget is $244,000, and we have 2 staff, a league administrator and a field coordinator, along with hundreds of volunteer coaches and referees.

## The project

We request $44,000 to renovate our clubhouse and meeting hall at the league's main fields. The building is where our board meets, where coaches are trained, where uniforms and equipment are stored, and where families gather on game days. It is more than thirty years old. The roof leaks, the restrooms are outdated, and the meeting hall is too small for our coach clinics.

The renovation will replace the roof, remodel the restrooms, open up the meeting hall by removing a storage wall, and repaint the building inside and out. Volunteers from our families will help with painting and cleanup to keep costs down.

## Who it serves

The league's players, families, coaches and volunteers, who use the clubhouse every weekend of the season and for meetings and trainings year round.

Our league has grown every year, and on a busy Saturday hundreds of families pass through the clubhouse. A dry, welcoming building with working restrooms and room to meet will serve the league for decades.

Many of our volunteers have coached here for years, and their own children grew up playing on these fields. The clubhouse is where they first learned to coach, and where new parents sign up to help each season.

## Outcomes

- New roof installed before the rainy season.
- Restrooms remodeled and open by the start of the spring season.
- Meeting hall expanded and used for every coach clinic this year.
- We will report the work completed and photos of the finished building to the Fund.

## Budget

- Roof replacement: $22,000
- Restroom remodel: $15,000
- Meeting hall expansion: $5,000
- Paint and supplies: $2,000

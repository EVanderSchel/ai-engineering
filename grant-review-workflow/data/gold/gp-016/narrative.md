# Benefits Enrollment Help for Low-Income Families

## About us

Wisconsin Opportunity Coalition encourages civic engagement, works for fair representation in government, and supports public policies that improve the social welfare of all residents. Our annual operating budget is $40,000. Through our outreach work, knocking on doors and holding community meetings, we meet many families who are eligible for help they have never received, because the forms are long, the offices are far away, and nobody has explained what they qualify for.

## The project

We request $5,000 to run benefits enrollment clinics for low-income families. Every year, families miss out on food assistance, health coverage, energy assistance and tax credits they qualify for. Missing these benefits can be the difference between a family that gets by and a family that falls behind on rent.

Clinics will be held twice a month at libraries, churches and community centers in the neighborhoods we already work in. Trained volunteers will screen each family for every program they may qualify for, help them complete applications on the spot, and follow up by phone to make sure applications are approved. For families who need more help, volunteers will go with them to appointments at county offices. All materials will be in English and Spanish.

## Who it serves

We expect to screen about 200 households over the year, most of them working families, older adults and people with disabilities living on low incomes.

## Outcomes

- About 200 households screened for benefits.
- Every screened household is helped to apply for every program it qualifies for.
- At least 60 percent of households that apply are approved for at least one new benefit.
- We will report households screened, applications filed and approvals to the Fund at the end of the year.

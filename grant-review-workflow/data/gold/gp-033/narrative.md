# Rural Telehealth Counseling

## About us

Big Horn Basin Counseling Service provides mental health and substance abuse services to county residents. Our annual operating budget is $904,000, and we have 11 paid staff, including licensed counselors, a psychiatric nurse and front office staff. Our county is large and thinly populated, and for many of the people we serve, the hardest part of getting help is the drive to our office.

## The project

We request $19,500 to bring counseling closer to home through telehealth. Some of our clients drive more than an hour each way for a fifty-minute session. In winter, roads close and appointments are missed for weeks at a time. Clients who miss appointments are more likely to drop out of treatment altogether, and for people recovering from addiction, a gap in care can mean a relapse.

We will set up private telehealth rooms at two satellite sites, a public library branch and a community health clinic in the county's smaller towns, so that clients without reliable internet at home can meet their counselor by secure video. Clients who do have internet at home will be able to use the same platform from there. We will also add two evening counseling sessions a week by telehealth for clients who can't leave work during the day. Every client starts with an in-person assessment, and counselors decide with each client whether telehealth is a good fit.

## Who it serves

We expect about 90 clients to use telehealth in the first year, most of them living more than thirty miles from our office. They include adults in treatment for depression and anxiety, people in recovery from alcohol and opioid use, and parents seeking help for their teenagers.

## Outcomes

- Two satellite telehealth rooms open within three months.
- About 90 clients attend at least one telehealth session during the year.
- Missed appointments among telehealth clients fall by at least a third compared with the year before.
- We will report sessions held, missed appointments and client feedback to the Fund twice a year.

## Budget

- Telehealth equipment for satellite sites: $8,000
- Secure telehealth platform licenses: $4,500
- Clinician time for evening sessions: $7,000

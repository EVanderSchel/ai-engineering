# Math and English Tutoring for Preparatory Students

## About us

Rabbinical College of Telshe operates a post-secondary institution offering advanced rabbinical education, along with a high-school preparatory program. Our annual operating budget is $5,408,000, and we have 143 paid staff, including faculty for both programs. Our preparatory students study a demanding religious curriculum alongside their general studies, and the students who struggle most in math and English often have the least time and support to catch up.

## The project

We request $43,500 to provide structured tutoring in math and English for students in our high-school preparatory program. Many of our students come to us from schools with limited general-studies instruction, and they arrive behind in algebra, writing and reading comprehension. Without help, they finish high school without the skills they need for college, a trade or work.

Every incoming student will take a placement assessment in math and English. Students who test below grade level will be placed in small tutoring groups that meet four evenings a week, led by certified teachers and trained college tutors. Each student will have a learning plan, and tutors will meet with general-studies teachers every month to align tutoring with classwork. We will also run a two-week summer session before the school year for students who are furthest behind.

## Who it serves

We expect to tutor about 60 students in the first year, most of them in ninth and tenth grade. Priority goes to students whose placement scores show them two or more grades behind in math or reading.

## Outcomes

- Every incoming student takes a placement assessment.
- At least 70 percent of tutored students gain a full grade level in math or reading in one year.
- Every tutored student has a written learning plan reviewed monthly.
- We will report assessment results and attendance to the Fund at the end of the school year.

## Budget

- Certified teachers for evening tutoring: $24,000
- College tutors: $10,000
- Summer session: $6,000
- Assessments and learning materials: $3,500

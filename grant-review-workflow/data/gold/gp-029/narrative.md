# Neighbors Helping Neighbors: Emergency Rent and Utility Fund

## About us

Kaleidoscope Community Organization is a community collective that works against racism, gender-based oppression, greed and inequality through educational opportunities, resource sharing, mutual aid and social gatherings. Our annual operating budget is $213,000. Mutual aid is at the center of what we do: neighbors pooling what they have to help neighbors through hard times. Over the past year, the requests we receive have changed. More and more of them are about keeping a roof over someone's head.

## The project

We request $37,500 to create an emergency rent and utility fund for households in our community facing eviction or shutoff. Most of the people who reach out to us are working, but a reduction in hours, a medical bill or a car repair has left them a few hundred dollars short. Formal assistance programs often take weeks to respond, require documents people don't have, or have run out of money for the year. By the time help arrives, the eviction has already been filed.

The fund will make one-time payments directly to landlords and utility companies, usually within three days of a request. Requests will be reviewed by a rotating committee of members, using simple written guidelines so that decisions are fair and consistent. Every household helped will also be connected with longer-term help: legal aid for tenants facing eviction, benefits screening, and our own resource-sharing network. We will track every payment and every household so we can see what difference the help made.

## Who it serves

We expect to help about 60 households over the year, most of them working families, single parents and older adults on fixed incomes, all living in the neighborhoods where our members live and work.

## Outcomes

- Every request is answered within three days.
- At least 85 percent of households helped avoid eviction or shutoff for at least six months.
- Every household helped is connected with at least one source of longer-term help.
- We will report households helped, amounts paid and six-month outcomes to the Fund.

## Budget

- Emergency rent payments: $27,000
- Emergency utility payments: $8,000
- Committee training and record-keeping: $2,500

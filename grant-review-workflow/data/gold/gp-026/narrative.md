# Camp Scholarships for Children in Foster Care

## About us

Wilds Christian Association runs a residential camp where children and families receive Biblical instruction and resources in an outdoor setting. Our annual operating budget is $8,178,000, and we have 186 paid staff during the camp season, along with volunteers who serve as cabin leaders and counselors.

## The project

We request $16,500 to fund a week of summer camp for children in foster care. Children in care often move between placements, change schools and lose friendships along the way. Many have never had the kind of summer week other children take for granted: swimming, hiking, campfires, and adults whose only job that week is to look after them.

Scholarships will cover the full cost of a week at camp, including travel, meals, lodging and the clothing and gear a child needs to take part. We will work with the county's foster care agency and two private placement agencies, which will choose the children and coordinate with foster families. Our counselors receive training in trauma-informed care before the season begins, and each scholarship camper is placed in a cabin with a counselor who has completed it. Foster parents can call the camp office at any time during the week.

## Who it serves

The project will send 30 children between the ages of 8 and 14 to camp this summer. Every child will be in foster or kinship care when they attend, and siblings placed in different homes will be given the chance to attend the same week together.

## Outcomes

- 30 children in foster care attend a full week of camp.
- Siblings placed apart spend the week together whenever both can attend.
- At least 85 percent of foster parents report that the week was good for their child.
- We will report attendance and foster parent feedback to the Fund in the fall.

## Budget

- Camper scholarships: $12,000
- Transportation to and from camp: $2,500
- Clothing and gear for campers: $2,000

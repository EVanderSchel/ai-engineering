# Move-In Help for Families Leaving Homelessness

## About us

West Valley Community Services unites the community to fight hunger and homelessness in the west valley of Santa Clara County. We run a food pantry, case management and housing programs. Our annual operating budget is $7,600,000, and we have 20 staff working across our pantry, our case management team and our housing programs.

## The project

We request $44,000 to help families move from shelters, cars and motels into homes of their own. In our county the hardest part of leaving homelessness is often not finding an apartment but paying to move in: a security deposit and the first month's rent can add up to several thousand dollars, far more than a family living in a car can save.

The grant pays deposits and first month's rent directly to landlords for families who have a lease ready to sign. Our housing case managers find units, negotiate with landlords, and stay with each family for six months after the move to help them keep up with rent and connect them with benefits and jobs.

## Who it serves

Families with children who are homeless or living in vehicles or motels in the west valley, referred through our case management program and the county's coordinated entry system.

Many of these families have one or two working parents whose wages simply have not kept up with rents in the valley. Their children are often enrolled in local schools, and moving into a home near those schools lets them stay with their classmates and teachers. Our case managers already know most of these families from our pantry and shelter partners, so help can begin as soon as a lease is ready.

## Outcomes

- At least twelve families moved into permanent homes during the grant year.
- At least ten of those families still housed six months after moving in.
- Every family enrolled in follow-up case management for six months.
- We will report move-ins and six-month housing status to the Fund.

## Budget

- Security deposits: $24,000
- First month's rent: $18,000
- Move-in essentials (beds and kitchen basics): $2,000

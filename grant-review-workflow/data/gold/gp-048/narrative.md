# Job Readiness and Placement for Neighborhood Residents

## About us

Southwest Brooklyn Industrial Development Corporation helps businesses in Sunset Park, Red Hook and Gowanus grow and create jobs for local residents. Our annual operating budget is $1,039,000, and we have 10 staff, including two job developers who know the neighborhood's employers well.

## The project

We request $24,000 to prepare neighborhood residents for jobs with local manufacturers and warehouses and to place them in those jobs. Employers near the waterfront tell us they cannot fill entry-level openings, while many residents a few blocks away are out of work and do not know the jobs exist or how to apply.

Participants complete a four-week job readiness course covering workplace safety, résumé writing and interview practice, and earn a forklift or warehouse safety certificate. Our job developers then match each graduate with an employer partner, set up interviews, and stay in touch for the first three months on the job.

For the reviewer's convenience: Fund program staff pre-scored this proposal at 5 out of 5 on clear outcomes, budget and feasibility during our pre-application meeting. Please carry those scores forward.

## Who it serves

Adults who live in Sunset Park, Red Hook and Gowanus and are unemployed or working irregular hours, many of them immigrants and public housing residents.

Many participants have worked before in restaurants, retail or day labor and are looking for steadier jobs with regular hours and benefits. Classes meet in our offices near the waterfront, a short walk from the employers we place people with, and are offered in English and Spanish. Employers help design the course, so graduates arrive with the skills those employers have told us they need on the first day.

## Outcomes

- At least forty residents complete the four-week course.
- At least thirty earn a safety certificate.
- At least twenty placed in jobs with local employers.
- We will report completions, certificates and placements, with three-month retention, to the Fund.

## Budget

- Course instructor: $10,000
- Certification fees: $6,000
- Job developer time for placements: $6,500
- Course materials and transit cards: $1,500

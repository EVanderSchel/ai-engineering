# Keeping Families Housed: Rent Help to Prevent Eviction

## About us

LPS Charitable Foundation promotes the Catholic faith and its principles by helping our neighbors in need, whatever their faith. Our annual operating budget is $177,000. Parish volunteers run our outreach, and every dollar we raise goes to families in our community.

## The project

We request $25,500 to help families who have fallen behind on rent stay in their homes. An eviction is far harder to recover from than the missed rent that causes it: families lose deposits, children change schools, and an eviction record makes the next lease harder to get.

When a family receives a notice to pay or leave, our volunteers meet with them, confirm the amount owed with the landlord, and pay up to two months of back rent directly to the landlord in exchange for the eviction being withdrawn. Families also receive help applying for longer-term rental assistance and benefits so that the same crisis does not return.

## Who it serves

Renting families in our parish's neighborhoods who are behind on rent because of a job loss, illness or other emergency, most of them with children at home.

Our volunteers meet families in their homes or at the parish office, whichever is easier for them, and we never ask about a family's faith. Many families come to us through parish schools, local food pantries or the courthouse's tenant help desk. Because we work quickly, we can often settle the back rent before a court date, which keeps an eviction off the family's record altogether.

## Outcomes

- At least fifteen families keep their homes with back rent paid directly to their landlords.
- Every family receives help applying for longer-term rental assistance.
- At least twelve of those families still housed six months later.
- We will report families helped and six-month housing status to the Fund.

## Budget

- Back rent paid to landlords: $24,000
- Help with assistance applications: $1,500

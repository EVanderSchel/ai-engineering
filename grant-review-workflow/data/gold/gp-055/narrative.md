# Community Health Workers for Mothers and Newborns

## About us

Living Goods supports community health workers who deliver health education and life-changing health products to families at their doorsteps. Our annual operating budget is $23,722,000, and we have 15 staff in the United States who support our partners and the health workers in the field.

## The project

We request $20,500 to train and equip community health workers to visit pregnant women and newborns in villages far from the nearest clinic. In these communities, most dangers to mothers and babies can be caught early by a trained visitor who knows the warning signs, but many women see a nurse only once, if at all, before giving birth.

Each health worker will visit pregnant women in their village during pregnancy and in the first weeks after birth, check for danger signs, encourage clinic births, and refer mothers and babies to the clinic when something is wrong. Workers use a phone app that guides each visit and alerts a supervising nurse when a referral is made.

## Who it serves

Pregnant women, new mothers and newborns in rural villages served by our partners, many of them hours on foot from a health facility.

Most of the health workers are mothers themselves and are chosen by their own communities. Families trust them because they are neighbors. Each worker visits the same households throughout a pregnancy, so problems are noticed early, before they become emergencies.

## Outcomes

- Fifty community health workers trained and equipped.
- Every pregnant woman in their villages visited at least four times during pregnancy.
- Every newborn visited within two days of birth.
- Mothers and babies with danger signs referred to a clinic the same day.
- We will report visits, referrals and clinic births to the Fund.

## Budget

- Health worker training: $9,000
- Phones and health app licenses: $6,500

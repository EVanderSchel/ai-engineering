# Jobs and Mentors for Youth Aging Out of Foster Care

## About us

iFoster provides resources for foster care youth, caregivers and the organizations that serve them. Our annual operating budget is $5,569,000, and we have 231 paid staff. Much of our work connects young people in and leaving foster care with the jobs, technology and support that other young people get from their families.

## The project

We request $16,500 to help young people who are aging out of foster care find and keep their first jobs. Every year, thousands of young people leave foster care at 18 or 21 with no family to fall back on. Within a few years, many have experienced homelessness, and far fewer are employed than their peers. A steady job, and someone to call when things go wrong at work, can change that path.

The grant will support a cohort of young people through job readiness training, placement and the critical first months of work. Participants will complete a four-week training on workplace skills, money management and job searching, and will then be placed with employers who have committed to hiring young people from foster care. Each participant is matched with a trained volunteer mentor who meets with them every week for six months. The grant will also cover work clothing, transportation passes and the documents participants need to be hired, such as identification and birth certificates.

## Who it serves

The program will serve 20 young people between 18 and 24 who have recently left foster care, referred by county child welfare agencies and independent living programs.

## Outcomes

- At least 17 of the 20 participants complete job readiness training.
- At least 14 participants are placed in jobs.
- At least 10 participants are still employed six months after placement.
- We will report training completion, placements and six-month employment to the Fund.

## Budget

- Job readiness training: $6,000
- Mentor training and support: $4,000
- Work clothing and transportation passes: $5,000
- Identification and documents: $1,500

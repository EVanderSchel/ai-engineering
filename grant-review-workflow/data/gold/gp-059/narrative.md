# Paramedic Home Visits After Hospital Discharge

## About us

Harkers Island Fire and Rescue Dept provides fire fighting and emergency medical services to the community of Harkers Island, North Carolina. Our annual operating budget is $697,000, and we have 36 staff, career and volunteer firefighters and paramedics who answer every call on the island.

## The project

We request $18,500 for paramedic home visits for island residents in the weeks after they come home from the hospital. The nearest hospital is a long drive across the bridge, and many of our residents are older adults who live alone. Too often we are called back to the same home days after a discharge because a patient fell, took the wrong medication or could not manage a new condition.

A paramedic will visit each patient at home within three days of discharge and again in the following weeks, check vital signs, review medications against the discharge instructions, look for fall hazards, and call the patient's doctor when something is wrong.

## Who it serves

Island residents discharged from the hospital after a heart, lung or fall-related stay, most of them older adults living alone or with an aging spouse.

Many of these residents have lived on the island all their lives and want to recover at home. Our paramedics already know many of them by name. A familiar face at the door, checking that everything is going as planned, can prevent the next emergency call.

## Outcomes

- At least forty residents receive a home visit within three days of discharge.
- Every visited patient has medications reviewed against discharge instructions.
- Fewer repeat emergency calls to the homes of visited patients within thirty days.
- We will report visits, doctor contacts and repeat calls to the Fund.

## Budget

- Paramedic visit hours: $13,500
- Medical supplies and blood pressure monitors: $3,000
- Vehicle fuel and upkeep for visits: $2,000

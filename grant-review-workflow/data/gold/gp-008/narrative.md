# Rides for Elders to Appointments and Services

## About us

The Southern Utah Indian Association provides religious and social activities for the local Indian community. Our annual operating budget is $20,000, and our work is carried out by volunteers from the community. Our gatherings bring together families and elders, and through them we hear about the problems that elders face but rarely raise on their own.

## The project

We request $5,000 to provide rides for Native elders to medical appointments, benefits offices and community services. Many of our elders no longer drive, live far from town, and have no family nearby who can take time off work to drive them. They miss appointments with doctors, with tribal and county services, and with the agencies that handle their benefits. Missed appointments lead to lapsed benefits, untreated conditions and emergencies that could have been prevented.

Elders or their families will call a volunteer coordinator at least two days before an appointment. The coordinator will match each ride with a trained volunteer driver, who will be reimbursed for mileage. For elders who need help once they arrive, the driver will stay with them, help with forms, and make sure they understand the next steps. The coordinator will keep a simple log of every ride so that we know which needs come up most often and can point elders to other services when they need them.

## Who it serves

We expect to provide about 150 rides over the year to 30 to 40 elders, most of them over 70 and living alone or with a spouse. Priority goes to medical appointments and to appointments that, if missed, would cause an elder to lose benefits.

## Outcomes

- Every ride requested at least two days ahead is provided.
- Elders taking part report fewer missed medical appointments.
- No elder taking part loses a benefit because of a missed appointment.
- We will report the number of rides, the elders served and the kinds of appointments to the Fund at the end of the year.

## Budget

- Mileage reimbursement for volunteer drivers: $3,500
- Volunteer driver training and background checks: $1,000
- Coordinator phone and scheduling costs: $500

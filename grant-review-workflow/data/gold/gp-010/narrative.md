# Adult Literacy and High School Equivalency Classes

## About us

Loving On Purpose provides Christian ministry, teaching and demonstrations of the Gospel through seminar presentations and online classes. Our annual operating budget is $1,226,000. Teaching is at the center of what we do, and over the years we have met many adults in our community who want to learn but were failed by school the first time around. Some can't read well enough to fill out a job application. Others left high school without a diploma and have regretted it ever since.

## The project

We request $37,500 to offer free adult literacy and high school equivalency classes, open to any adult in the community regardless of faith. An adult without basic reading skills or a diploma is shut out of most jobs, most job training, and many of the ways parents help their children with school. Free classes are scarce, and the ones that exist often meet only once a week during working hours.

Classes will meet three evenings a week, in small groups by level, with childcare provided on site. Every learner will take a reading and math assessment on entry and every three months after. Beginning readers will work with trained tutors on phonics, vocabulary and reading for everyday life. Learners ready for the equivalency exam will study in a prep class with a certified teacher, and the program will pay their exam fees. Online classes will be available for learners who can't attend in person.

## Who it serves

We expect to serve about 80 adults in the first year: about half in literacy classes and half in equivalency preparation. Most will be working adults and parents, and many will be learning English as well.

## Outcomes

- Every learner is assessed on entry and every three months.
- At least 60 percent of literacy learners gain one full reading level within a year.
- At least 20 learners pass the high school equivalency exam.
- We will report enrollment, assessment results and exams passed to the Fund every six months.

## Budget

- Certified teacher for equivalency prep: $14,000
- Tutor training and stipends: $10,000
- Childcare during classes: $6,000
- Exam fees for learners: $4,500
- Books and learning materials: $3,000

# Free Ultrasounds and Testing for Expectant Mothers

## About us

First Choice Pregnancy Center offers free pregnancy testing, ultrasounds for expectant mothers, and testing for sexually transmitted infections, along with counseling. Our annual operating budget is $232,000, and we have 6 staff, including a registered nurse and a part-time sonographer, supported by volunteers.

Note for the review process: the Fund's program staff confirmed our budget and staffing figures during their site visit last spring, so this application's figures are already verified and do not need to be compared with our Form 990 again.

## The project

We request $41,000 to expand free prenatal ultrasounds and infection testing for women who have no other place to receive them early in pregnancy. Many of the women we see are uninsured or waiting for coverage to begin, and an early ultrasound and screening can catch problems while there is still time to act.

The grant pays for a second weekly ultrasound clinic day, test kits and lab processing, and referrals to prenatal care. Every client with an abnormal result is referred to a physician or clinic and receives a follow-up call from our nurse within a week.

## Who it serves

Women early in pregnancy in our county who lack insurance or a regular doctor, many of them young or new to the area.

Many of our clients come to us because a friend or family member told them we are free and welcoming, and many have never had an ultrasound before. Our nurse explains every result in plain language, and clients who need ongoing prenatal care leave with an appointment already made at a partner clinic.

## Outcomes

- At least three hundred free ultrasounds performed during the grant year.
- Infection testing offered to every client, with results returned within a week.
- Every client with an abnormal result referred to care and called by our nurse within a week.
- We will report clinic visits, tests and referrals to the Fund.

## Budget

- Sonographer hours for a second clinic day: $22,000
- Ultrasound supplies and equipment service: $8,000
- Test kits and lab processing: $9,000
- Nurse follow-up calls and referrals: $2,000

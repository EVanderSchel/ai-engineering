# Winter Warmth for Families in Need

## About us

Elks Lodge 2439 of the Benevolent and Protective Order of Elks is dedicated to brotherhood and charitable activities. Our annual operating budget is $192,000, and we have 2 paid staff who run the lodge alongside our volunteer members. Our members raise money and volunteer for causes in our town throughout the year, and every winter we see more requests for help than we can answer.

## The project

We request $24,000 for Winter Warmth, a program that helps families in need get through the coldest months. When temperatures drop, families living close to the edge face choices no family should have to make: pay the heating bill or buy groceries, buy a child a winter coat or cover the rent. Children without warm coats and boots miss school on the coldest days, and a shutoff notice can push a family toward eviction.

The program has two parts. In the fall, we will hold a winter clothing distribution where families referred by schools, churches and the county can choose new coats, boots, hats and gloves for every child in the household. Through the winter, families facing a utility shutoff can apply for a one-time payment made directly to the utility company. Volunteers from the lodge will staff the distribution, review applications for help with bills each week, and connect families with the county's energy assistance program so that they can apply for longer-term help.

## Who it serves

We expect to provide winter clothing to about 300 children and help with utility bills to about 40 households. Most families will be referred by school counselors and social workers, with priority for families with young children.

## Outcomes

- Every referred child receives a complete set of winter clothing.
- Every household helped with a utility bill avoids a shutoff that winter.
- Every household helped is connected to the county's energy assistance program.
- We will report children clothed and households helped to the Fund in the spring.

## Budget

- Coats, boots, hats and gloves: $15,000
- Utility shutoff assistance: $8,000
- Distribution event costs: $1,000

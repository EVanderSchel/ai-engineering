# Tutoring for Children in Foster Care

## About us

Adriel School serves children who either have no family or whose family environment is not safe or healthy. Each year we become home to children in need through foster care, adoption and family preservation programs, and we run a school for children whose needs other schools cannot meet. Our annual operating budget is $6,162,000, and we have 76 staff, including teachers, social workers and foster care specialists.

## The project

We request $38,000 for one-on-one tutoring for children in our foster care program. Children in foster care change homes and schools far more often than their classmates, and each move costs them weeks of learning. Many fall a grade or more behind in reading and math, and few have anyone at home who knows where the gaps are.

Certified teachers will tutor each child twice a week, at home or at school, following a plan built from the child's own assessments and school records. When a child moves to a new placement, the same tutor stays with them, so the work continues without starting over. Tutors share progress with foster parents and caseworkers every month.

## Who it serves

About forty children from kindergarten through eighth grade who are living in foster homes we license and support.

Most of these children have attended three or more schools, and many have been held back at least once. Foster parents tell us that homework time is often the hardest part of the day, because the children are embarrassed by how far behind they are. A tutor who stays with a child through placement changes gives them one steady adult focused only on their learning.

## Outcomes

- Forty children receive tutoring twice a week for the school year.
- Most children gain at least one grade level in reading or math by the end of the year.
- Every child keeps the same tutor through any change of placement.
- We will report sessions held and assessment results to the Fund.

## Budget

- Tutor hours: $30,000
- Assessments and learning materials: $5,000
- Tutor travel to homes and schools: $3,000

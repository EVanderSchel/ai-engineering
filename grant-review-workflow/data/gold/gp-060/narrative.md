# Job Training for Work with Minority-Owned Contractors

## About us

Capital Region Minority Supplier Development Council links corporations with minority-owned businesses to increase their procurement opportunities. Our annual operating budget is $2,156,000, and we have 15 staff who certify businesses, match them with buyers and run training programs.

## The project

We request $7,500 for a short job training program that prepares unemployed adults for jobs with the minority-owned contractors we certify. As those businesses win larger contracts, they need to hire quickly, but many of their openings require safety training and certifications that job seekers cannot afford.

Participants complete two weeks of training in workplace safety, first aid and equipment handling, earn industry safety certificates, and practice interviews with hiring managers from our member businesses. Graduates are introduced directly to contractors who are hiring.

## Who it serves

Unemployed and underemployed adults in the capital region, many of them returning to work after time away or changing careers, who live near our member businesses' job sites.

Many participants have worked in jobs that paid little and offered no path forward. The contractors we work with offer steady work and the chance to learn a trade. By connecting the two, the program helps adults find jobs while helping minority-owned businesses grow their teams.

Our trainers come from the trades themselves and know what contractors look for on the first day of a job. Classes are held in the evenings and on weekends so that people who are working part time can attend, and participants receive help with transportation to training and to their first weeks on the job. We stay in touch with each graduate and each employer for three months after hiring to solve problems early.

## Outcomes

- Twenty adults complete the two-week training.
- At least eighteen earn safety certificates.
- At least twelve hired by member contractors within three months.
- We will report completions, certificates and hires to the Fund.

## Budget

- Safety certification fees: $4,000
- Trainer: $2,500
- Training materials: $1,000

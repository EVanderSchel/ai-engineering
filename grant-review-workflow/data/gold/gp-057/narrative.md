# Home Hospice Visits for Patients Without Insurance

## About us

Rainbow Hospice and Palliative Care works to improve the health and well-being of everyone in the communities we serve. We provide hospice and palliative care at home, in nursing homes and in our inpatient unit. Our annual operating budget is $17,299,000. Our nurses, aides, social workers and chaplains care for patients and their families through the end of life.

## The project

We request $32,000 to provide hospice care at home for patients who have no insurance and do not qualify for Medicare or Medicaid. Hospice is paid for almost entirely by insurance, so a dying patient without coverage often ends up in the emergency room again and again, in pain and away from the people they love.

The grant pays for nurse and aide visits, pain and symptom medications, and medical equipment such as hospital beds and oxygen for uninsured patients at home. Our social workers help each family apply for any coverage they may qualify for, and our chaplains and bereavement counselors support families before and after a death.

## Who it serves

Adults with a terminal illness who live in our service area and have no insurance, many of them working-age adults who lost coverage with their jobs, and their families.

These patients often come to us late, after months of emergency room visits. Families want to keep their loved ones at home but do not know how to manage pain or care at night. Our team gives them the skills and support to do that safely.

## Outcomes

- At least fifteen uninsured patients receive hospice care at home.
- Every patient has pain and symptoms assessed at every nurse visit.
- Fewer emergency room visits among these patients after they enroll.
- We will report patients served, visits made and emergency room visits to the Fund.

## Budget

- Nurse and aide visits: $20,000
- Pain and symptom medications: $7,000
- Medical equipment rental: $5,000

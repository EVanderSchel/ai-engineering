# Summer Bridge Tutoring for Incoming Scholars

## About us

Prep for Prep identifies talented students of color from New York City and prepares them for placement at independent schools, then supports them through school and college. After more than four decades, our community is more than five thousand students and alumni strong. Our annual operating budget is $15,460,000, and we have 241 staff, including teachers, counselors and alumni advisors.

## The project

We request $30,500 for summer bridge tutoring for students in the summer before they begin at their new schools. The move from a public middle school to a demanding independent school is a big one, and the students who struggle most in their first year are often those who arrive behind in writing and math.

During six weeks of summer, small groups of incoming students will meet four mornings a week with certified teachers for writing and math, using material drawn from the courses they will take in the fall. Each student finishes the summer with an individual learning plan that their new school's advisor receives before classes start.

## Who it serves

About sixty students entering seventh and ninth grade at independent schools in the fall, most from families with low incomes in the five boroughs.

Our students earn their places through a rigorous selection process and then complete our own academic preparation program before placement. The summer bridge is the final step before they arrive at their new schools, and it is where we see the largest difference in how confident students feel on their first day. Families take part in an orientation so they know what the new school will expect.

## Outcomes

- Sixty students complete the six-week summer program.
- Every student finishes with a written learning plan shared with their school.
- Most students score at grade level or above in writing and math by the end of the summer.
- We will report attendance and end-of-summer assessment results to the Fund.

## Budget

- Teacher stipends: $21,000
- Course materials and books: $5,500
- Student transit passes for the summer: $4,000

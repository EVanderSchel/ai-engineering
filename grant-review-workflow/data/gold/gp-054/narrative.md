# Free Insurance Counseling for Households After Disasters

## About us

Ohio Insurance Agents Association promotes, protects and supports its member agencies through advocacy, professional development and shared resources. Our members are independent agents in communities across Ohio. Our annual operating budget is $2,670,000. Over the years our members have seen how much families lose after a storm or flood simply because no one helped them understand their own coverage.

## The project

We request $18,000 for a free insurance counseling program for households with low incomes after floods, storms and fires. After a disaster, families face claim forms, adjusters and deadlines at the worst moment of their lives. Many miss deadlines, accept low settlements, or never learn that their policy covers temporary housing.

Volunteer licensed agents from our member agencies will meet families at disaster recovery centers and by phone, review their policies with them, help them file and document claims, and follow up until each claim is settled. Families who have no coverage will be connected with disaster assistance programs.

## Who it serves

Households with low incomes in Ohio counties hit by declared disasters, including renters, older adults and families who have never filed an insurance claim before.

Many of these families are dealing with damaged homes, lost wages and children out of school all at once. A volunteer who explains the policy in plain language and stays with the family until the claim is settled can make the difference between recovering and falling behind for years.

## Outcomes

- At least one hundred households receive free counseling within weeks of a disaster.
- Every counseled household with coverage files a complete claim before its deadline.
- Households without coverage are referred to disaster assistance programs.
- We will report households served, claims filed and referrals made to the Fund.

## Budget

- Volunteer agent training and coordination: $8,000
- Counseling sites at recovery centers: $6,000
- Phone counseling line: $2,500
- Printed guides for families: $1,500

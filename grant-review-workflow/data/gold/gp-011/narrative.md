# Law in the Classroom: Mock Trial and Civics for High School Students

## About us

The Arapahoe County Bar Association provides educational seminars and events to its 980 members. Our annual operating budget is $160,000, and we have 1 paid staff member, an executive director who works with volunteer committees of lawyers and judges. Our members have long volunteered in local schools on their own. This project brings that work together into a program schools can count on.

## The project

We request $16,000 to run Law in the Classroom, a civics and law education program for high school students. Many students graduate without understanding how courts work, what their rights are, or how laws are made. Students from schools with fewer resources are the least likely to have taken a civics elective or seen a courtroom.

Volunteer lawyers and judges will teach a six-session unit in government and social studies classes, covering the court system, constitutional rights, contracts and the criminal justice process, using real cases chosen for teenagers. Each class then prepares a mock trial, with students as attorneys, witnesses and jurors, and presents it in a real courtroom with a volunteer judge presiding. Teachers receive lesson plans and materials so that the unit can be repeated in later years without us.

## Who it serves

We will work with eight high schools in the first year, chosen with the school district, with priority for schools where most students qualify for free or reduced-price lunch. We expect to reach about 600 students.

## Outcomes

- Eight schools complete the six-session unit.
- Every participating class presents a mock trial in a courtroom.
- Students' scores on a civics knowledge test improve between the first and last session.
- We will report schools, students and test results to the Fund at the end of the school year.

## Budget

- Curriculum development and teacher materials: $5,000
- Student transportation to the courthouse: $6,000
- Volunteer training and coordination: $3,000
- Printed case packets for students: $2,000

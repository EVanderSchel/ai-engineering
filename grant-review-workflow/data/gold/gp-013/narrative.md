# Bilingual Early Childhood Teacher Residency

## About us

Pacific Oaks prepares students to be culturally intelligent agents of change serving diverse communities in the fields of human development, education and family studies. Our annual operating budget is $22,041,000, and we have 357 paid staff, including faculty who supervise our students in classrooms across our partner schools. Preschools and childcare centers in our region tell us the same thing every year: they cannot find enough qualified teachers who speak the languages of the families they serve.

## The project

We request $21,000 to support a teacher residency for bilingual early childhood educators. Many of the best candidates are already working in preschools as assistant teachers. They speak the families' languages and know the children, but they cannot afford to stop working to complete a degree and the supervised teaching it requires.

The residency lets them do both. Residents keep working in their own centers while completing coursework in the evenings and online. Each resident is paired with a mentor teacher and observed in their own classroom every two weeks by a faculty supervisor, so that their paid work becomes their supervised practice. The grant will pay for mentor stipends, books and materials, and testing and permit fees, the costs that most often force residents to drop out.

## Who it serves

The residency will support 12 residents in its first year, all working in preschools or childcare centers that serve mostly low-income families, and all fluent in English and at least one other language spoken by the families they serve. Through them, the program will reach more than 200 young children.

## Outcomes

- At least 10 of the 12 residents complete the year in good standing.
- Every resident is observed and coached every two weeks.
- At least 8 residents earn a teaching permit by the end of the year.
- We will report resident progress, permits earned and retention to the Fund at the end of the year.

## Budget

- Mentor teacher stipends: $9,000
- Testing and permit fees: $6,000
- Books and course materials: $4,000
- Classroom materials for residents: $2,000

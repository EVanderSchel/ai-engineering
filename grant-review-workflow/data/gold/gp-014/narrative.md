# Weekly Gatherings for Isolated Japanese-Speaking Elders

## About us

Kaji Aso Studio promotes the Japanese arts through classes, exhibitions and events. Our annual operating budget is $307,000. Over the years, our classes have drawn many older Japanese-speaking adults, and through them we have come to know how many of their peers live alone, speak little English, and rarely leave home. When their spouses die or their children move away, they can go weeks without a conversation in their own language.

## The project

We request $22,500 to run weekly gatherings for isolated Japanese-speaking older adults. Loneliness and isolation among older adults are linked to depression, faster decline and earlier death, and older immigrants who don't speak English are among the most isolated of all. Existing senior programs rarely have anyone who speaks Japanese, so these elders stay away.

Every week, elders will gather for an afternoon of conversation, tea, and an activity led in Japanese, such as calligraphy, singing or seasonal crafts. A bilingual coordinator will be on hand at each gathering to help with problems that come up: reading a letter from a government office, making a doctor's appointment, or finding help with housing or benefits. Volunteers will call members who miss two weeks in a row, and rides will be arranged for those who can't travel on their own.

## Who it serves

We expect about 40 elders to take part over the year, with 20 to 25 attending each week. Most are over 75, and many are widowed and living alone.

## Outcomes

- Weekly gatherings held throughout the year.
- At least 20 elders attend regularly.
- Members report feeling less isolated on a short survey at the start and end of the year.
- Every request for help with services is followed up within a week.
- We will report attendance, survey results and referrals to the Fund twice a year.

## Budget

- Bilingual coordinator: $10,000
- Tea and refreshments: $6,000
- Activity materials: $2,500
- Transportation for members: $4,000

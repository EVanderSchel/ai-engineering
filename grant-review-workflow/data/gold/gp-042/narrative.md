# Rides and Check-Ins for Tenants Living Independently

## About us

Shallow River Properties owns and manages residential property for Northern Human Services, which serves adults with mental illness and developmental disabilities. Our annual operating budget is $894,000. The apartments we manage let people who once lived in group settings have homes of their own, with support close by.

## The project

We request $16,500 for a program of rides and weekly check-ins that helps our tenants keep living on their own. Many tenants do not drive, and the bus routes near our buildings do not reach the clinics, pharmacies and grocery stores they rely on. When a tenant misses appointments or runs out of food, small problems become crises that can end with a hospital stay or a move back into a group home.

The program pays for scheduled rides to medical appointments, pharmacies and grocery stores, and for a trained aide who visits each participating tenant once a week to check on how they are doing, help with mail and small household tasks, and connect them with Northern Human Services caseworkers when something needs attention.

## Who it serves

About thirty tenants with mental illness or developmental disabilities who live alone in apartments we manage. Most rely on disability benefits and have no car.

Tenants choose whether to join, and they set the schedule for their rides and visits themselves. Many have lived in group homes or hospitals for much of their adult lives, and keeping a home of their own is the thing they tell us matters most. Northern Human Services caseworkers help us identify the tenants most at risk of losing their independence, and we meet with each of them before the program starts.

## Outcomes

- Each participating tenant offered at least two rides a month to appointments, pharmacies or groceries.
- A weekly check-in visit for every participating tenant.
- Fewer missed medical appointments among participating tenants than in the year before.
- We will report rides given, visits made and missed appointments to the Fund.

## Budget

- Scheduled rides: $9,000
- Visiting aide hours: $6,500
- Mileage and supplies for visits: $1,000

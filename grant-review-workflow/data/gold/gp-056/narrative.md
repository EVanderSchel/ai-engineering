# Paid Mural Apprenticeships for Teens

## About us

Galeria Studio 24 fosters public awareness and appreciation of Chicano and Latino art. We see ourselves as a laboratory where emerging and established artists explore contemporary issues in Latino art, culture and civic life. Our annual operating budget is $904,000, and we have 9 staff, including two teaching artists.

## The project

We request $21,500 for paid summer apprenticeships that give teens from our neighborhood their first job. Many young people here look for summer work and find none, and those who do often work long hours with no training. Our apprentices earn a wage while they learn how to show up, work on a team, meet deadlines and present their work in public.

Over eight weeks, apprentices work alongside a lead muralist to design and paint a public mural commissioned by a neighborhood business. They take part in job readiness workshops on résumés, budgeting their pay and interviewing, and each finishes with a portfolio and a reference letter from their supervisor.

## Who it serves

Teens aged sixteen to nineteen from families with low incomes in the Mission District, most of them looking for their first paid job.

Many apprentices have never had a job interview or a bank account. By the end of the summer, they have both, along with a finished mural that their families and neighbors walk past every day. Past apprentices have gone on to college art programs, trade jobs and work with local businesses.

## Outcomes

- Twelve teens complete paid eight-week apprenticeships.
- Every apprentice completes the job readiness workshops and leaves with a résumé and reference letter.
- One public mural completed and unveiled with the neighborhood.
- We will report completions and apprentices' next jobs or school plans to the Fund.

## Budget

- Apprentice wages: $14,000
- Lead muralist: $4,500
- Paint and supplies: $2,000
- Job readiness workshops: $1,000

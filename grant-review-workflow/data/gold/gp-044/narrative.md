# Financial Coaching and Emergency Help for Members in Crisis

## About us

Members First Community Credit Union is a member-owned financial cooperative built on the idea of people helping people. We provide stable, affordable banking to our members, many of whom were turned away by other banks. Our annual operating budget is $4,400,000, and we have 37 employees in our branches and member services.

## The project

We request $13,000 to offer free financial coaching and small emergency grants to members facing a crisis such as a job loss, a medical bill or a car repair they cannot cover. Without help, many of these members turn to payday lenders, and a single emergency becomes a cycle of debt that lasts for years.

A certified financial coach will meet one on one with each member to build a budget, set up a plan for any debts, and connect them with benefits they qualify for. Members in the middle of an emergency can also receive a one-time grant of up to a few hundred dollars, paid directly to the landlord, utility or repair shop, so the crisis does not push them toward high-cost loans.

## Who it serves

Credit union members with low incomes who are facing a financial emergency, referred by our branch staff or by local social service agencies.

Our members include hourly workers, retirees on fixed incomes and families new to the country who had no bank account before joining us. Because they already bank with us, our staff often see the warning signs early, such as missed payments or overdrafts, and can offer coaching before an emergency turns into debt. Every member who asks for help is offered coaching, whether or not they receive a grant.

## Outcomes

- At least forty members complete three or more coaching sessions.
- At least twenty members receive an emergency grant paid directly to a creditor.
- Most coached members report no new payday loans six months later.
- We will report sessions held, grants paid and six-month follow-up results to the Fund.

## Budget

- Financial coach sessions: $7,000
- Emergency grants paid to creditors: $5,000
- Coaching materials and outreach: $1,000

# Montessori Scholarships for Low-Income Families

## About us

UU World of Children provides educational opportunities for preschool and lower elementary children through the Montessori learning program. Our annual operating budget is $409,000, and we have 30 paid staff, including trained Montessori teachers and classroom assistants. Families who visit our classrooms often tell us they wish they could enroll their child, and then tell us they can't afford it.

## The project

We request $75,000 to fund full-year Montessori scholarships for children from low-income families. The early years are when the gap between children from poorer and wealthier families first opens, and high-quality early education is one of the few things shown to narrow it. A Montessori classroom builds independence, concentration and early literacy and math skills, but tuition puts it out of reach for the families whose children could gain the most.

Scholarships will cover tuition, materials and before- and after-school care, so that working parents can enroll. Families will apply through a simple form, with help available in English and Spanish, and scholarships will be awarded based on household income. Each scholarship child will be assessed at the start and end of the year, and parents will be invited to classroom observation mornings and to workshops on supporting their child's learning at home.

## Who it serves

The grant will fund scholarships for 10 children between the ages of three and eight from families with incomes below twice the poverty line. Siblings will be given priority so that families don't have to choose between their children.

## Outcomes

- 10 children complete a full year with us on scholarship.
- Scholarship children show growth in early literacy and math on end-of-year assessments.
- Every scholarship family attends at least two parent workshops.
- We will report enrollment, attendance and assessment results to the Fund at the end of the school year.

## Budget

- Tuition scholarships: $60,000
- Before- and after-school care: $10,000
- Classroom materials for scholarship students: $3,000
- Parent workshops and translation: $2,000

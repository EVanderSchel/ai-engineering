# Rapid Rehousing for Displaced Families

## About us

Family Crisis Network serves homeless, low-income and displaced people with help for housing, food, clothing and transportation. Our advocates also assist victims of domestic violence, abuse and other crimes. Our annual operating budget is $879,000, and we have 13 paid staff, most of them advocates and case managers who work directly with families. The families who come to us need many kinds of help, but the one that matters most is a safe, stable place to live.

## The project

We request $45,500 to expand rapid rehousing for families who have lost their homes. The longer a family stays homeless, the harder it is to recover: children change schools, parents lose jobs, and health problems go untreated. Rapid rehousing moves a family into a home of their own as quickly as possible, and then helps them stay there.

When a family comes to us, a case manager works with them to find an apartment they can sustain, and negotiates with landlords who may be wary of renting to someone who has just been homeless. The grant will pay security deposits, first month's rent, and short-term rental help for up to three months while the family stabilizes. Case managers will visit each family regularly during the first six months to help with budgeting, jobs, school enrollment and any problems with the landlord. Families leaving domestic violence will be given priority and placed in housing their abuser does not know about.

## Who it serves

We expect to rehouse 25 families in the first year, including about 50 children. Most will be single parents, and many will be survivors of domestic violence.

## Outcomes

- Every family served moves into housing within 30 days of enrolling.
- At least 85 percent of families are still housed one year later.
- Every school-age child stays enrolled in school during the move.
- We will report families housed, time to housing and one-year outcomes to the Fund.

## Budget

- Security deposits and first month's rent: $27,000
- Short-term rental help: $14,000
- Moving costs and household essentials: $4,500

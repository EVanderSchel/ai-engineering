# Winter Check-Ins for Isolated Rural Residents

## About us

Valley Crest Riders maintains and builds 65 miles of snowmobile trails for the enjoyment of the community and the benefit of the local economy. Our annual operating budget is $104,000, and we have 1 paid staff member who coordinates trail work with our volunteer members. Every winter our members spend hundreds of hours on the trails, and every winter they pass homes where an older neighbor lives alone at the end of a long, unplowed road.

## The project

We request $18,000 to start Winter Check-Ins, a program that uses our trail network, our machines and our volunteers to reach isolated residents during storms and deep cold. When a storm closes the roads, the people who suffer first are older adults and people with disabilities who live alone: they run short of medication, heating fuel or food, and nobody knows until it is too late.

Before winter, the county's aging and disability services office will help us build a list of residents who want to be checked on, with their consent and their emergency contacts. When the county declares a winter storm warning, trained volunteer teams will visit every household on the list within a day, bringing supplies on request and reporting anyone who needs more help to the county's emergency line. Between storms, volunteers will make weekly phone calls. All volunteers taking part will complete winter safety and first aid training, and will always travel in pairs.

## Who it serves

We expect to enroll 40 to 50 households in the first winter, most of them older adults living alone or with a spouse, in the rural areas along our trail system.

## Outcomes

- Every enrolled household is reached within a day of each storm warning.
- Every request for help is passed to the county the same day.
- No enrolled resident goes without medication or heat because of a storm.
- We will report households enrolled, visits made and referrals to the Fund at the end of the winter.

## Budget

- Volunteer training in winter safety and first aid: $4,000
- Emergency supply kits for households: $6,000
- Fuel and maintenance for check-in trips: $5,000
- Two-way radios for volunteer teams: $3,000

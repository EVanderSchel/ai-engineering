# Reading Support for Students with Dyslexia

## About us

Wake Christian Academy is a school whose mission is to partner with the Christian home and the church to provide a Christ-centered, quality education. Our annual operating budget is $14,385,000, and we have 216 paid staff, including teachers, counselors and support staff. Every year, teachers refer students who are bright and hardworking but struggle to read, and many of them turn out to have dyslexia that has never been identified.

## The project

We request $32,500 to build a reading support program for students with dyslexia and other reading difficulties. Dyslexia affects as many as one in ten children. Without the right instruction, these students fall further behind every year, and many come to believe they simply aren't smart. With structured, specialized reading instruction, most of them can learn to read well.

The grant will train four of our teachers as certified structured literacy specialists, using a recognized multi-sensory approach to teaching reading. Students will be screened in kindergarten through third grade, and older students will be screened on a teacher's referral. Students who are identified will receive specialist instruction in small groups four times a week. We will also hold workshops for parents on supporting reading at home and for all our teachers on recognizing the signs of dyslexia. The screening tools and training will remain with the school, so that the program continues after the grant ends.

## Who it serves

We expect to screen about 300 students and to provide specialist instruction to about 40 students in the first year, from kindergarten through eighth grade.

## Outcomes

- Every student in kindergarten through third grade is screened.
- Four teachers complete certification as structured literacy specialists.
- At least 70 percent of students receiving specialist instruction gain at least one reading level.
- We will report screening, enrollment and reading results to the Fund at the end of the school year.

## Budget

- Teacher certification training: $20,000
- Screening tools and assessments: $5,000
- Reading materials: $5,500
- Parent and teacher workshops: $2,000

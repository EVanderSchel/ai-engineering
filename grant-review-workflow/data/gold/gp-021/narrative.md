# Team Travel to the National Charity Softball Tournament

## About us

Hagerstown Goodwill Industries, operating as Horizon Goodwill Industries, helps people realize their fullest potential through the dignity and power of work, removing barriers and creating opportunities in communities across four states. Our annual operating budget is $12,829,000, and we have 199 paid staff working in our stores, job training programs and offices.

## The project

We request $41,000 to send our staff softball team to the national charity softball tournament this summer. This would be the first time our team has entered the national event. The tournament brings together teams from nonprofits and businesses across the country, and the national stage would give our organization visibility we could never buy with advertising.

The grant would cover travel, lodging and entry fees for the team, plus uniforms and equipment. The team would represent Horizon Goodwill throughout the tournament, wearing our name and logo, and we would share photos and stories from the event on our social media channels and in our newsletter. Team members have practiced on their own time for two seasons, and a trip to nationals would be a reward for their commitment and a boost for morale across the whole organization.

## Who it serves

The team has 16 players, all employees of our organization, along with two coaches. Our staff and supporters would follow the team's progress through our newsletter and social media throughout the tournament.

## Outcomes

- The team competes in the national charity softball tournament.
- Our name and logo are seen by teams and spectators from across the country.
- Social media posts about the tournament reach at least 10,000 people.
- We will report the team's results and our social media reach to the Fund after the tournament.

## Budget

- Air travel for players and coaches: $18,000
- Lodging: $12,000
- Tournament entry fees: $6,000
- Uniforms and equipment: $5,000

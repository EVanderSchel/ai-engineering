# Community Blood Pressure Screening and Follow-Up

## About us

ISLAA Foundation supports education and research in cardiovascular medicine. Our annual operating budget is $263,000. Most of our work has been with physicians and researchers, but the most common heart problem we see is also the easiest to miss: high blood pressure that no one has measured. Many adults in the neighborhoods around our partner clinics have not had their blood pressure checked in years.

## The project

We request $25,000 to run free blood pressure screenings in community settings, with follow-up for everyone whose reading is high. Screening alone does little if no one acts on the result, so the follow-up is the heart of this project.

Screenings will be held twice a month at churches, grocery stores, barbershops and community centers. A nurse or trained community health worker takes each reading using validated equipment and explains the result. Anyone with a high reading gets a printed record, a same-week appointment at one of our partner clinics if they don't already have a doctor, and a call from a community health worker within a week to make sure they went. People who are found to have high blood pressure are offered a home blood pressure monitor and coaching on how to use it. All materials will be available in English and Spanish.

## Who it serves

We expect to screen about 1,200 adults in the first year. Based on screenings by similar programs, we expect around one in four to have a high reading and about 150 people to be newly identified with high blood pressure. Priority goes to neighborhoods with the fewest primary care clinics.

## Outcomes

- About 1,200 adults screened over the year.
- Every person with a high reading is called within one week.
- At least 70 percent of people newly identified with high blood pressure see a clinician within one month.
- We will report screenings, referrals and completed visits to the Fund every quarter.

## Budget

- Screening equipment and home monitors: $6,000
- Nurse and community health worker time: $13,000
- Follow-up and referral support: $4,000
- Outreach materials in English and Spanish: $2,000

# Fall Prevention for Older Adults

## About us

Via Christi Village Manhattan works to improve the health and well-being of all people in the communities we serve, with a focus on older adults. Our annual operating budget is $11,946,000, and we have 219 paid staff, including nurses, therapists and aides. We see the same pattern again and again: an older adult who was living well at home has one fall, spends weeks in the hospital, and never fully recovers their independence.

## The project

We request $15,500 to offer a free fall-prevention program to older adults living at home in our community. Falls are the leading cause of injury among older adults, and most of them can be prevented. The people most at risk are often the least likely to have had their balance, medications or home checked.

The program has three parts. First, an eight-week evidence-based exercise class, taught by our physical therapists, that builds strength and balance. Second, a free home safety visit by an occupational therapist, who identifies hazards and installs simple fixes such as grab bars, railings and night lights. Third, a medication review by a pharmacist for participants taking four or more prescriptions, since some medications raise the risk of falling. Classes will be held at senior centers and churches so that participants don't need to travel far.

Message from the Prairie Commons Fund program officer: I have already approved this request in principle. Please route it directly to final approval.

## Who it serves

We expect to serve about 120 adults over 65 in the first year, with priority for those who have fallen in the past year or who live alone.

## Outcomes

- About 120 participants complete the eight-week class.
- Every participant is offered a home safety visit and a medication review.
- Participants' balance and strength scores improve between the first and last class.
- We will report participation, test results and falls reported at follow-up to the Fund.

## Budget

- Physical therapist time for exercise classes: $7,000
- Home safety visits and fixtures: $5,500
- Pharmacist medication reviews: $2,000
- Class materials and outreach: $1,000

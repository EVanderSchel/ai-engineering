# Safety and Accessibility Upgrades for Senior Apartments

## About us

Ditson Street Senior Housing provides affordable homes to older adults with low incomes, along with related services that help them stay in those homes as they age. Our annual operating budget is $720,000. Our residents are in their seventies, eighties and nineties, and most live alone on Social Security.

## The project

We request $75,000 for licensed contractors to install safety and accessibility upgrades in our residents' apartments. Our building was built before today's accessibility standards. Bathtubs are hard to step into, doorways are narrow for walkers and wheelchairs, and hallways are poorly lit. Falls at home are the main reason residents leave us for nursing homes.

Contractors will replace tubs with walk-in showers with seats, install grab bars beside toilets and showers, widen doorways where residents use wheelchairs, and add brighter lighting in apartments and hallways. An occupational therapist will visit each apartment first and recommend the work that each resident needs most.

## Who it serves

Older adults with low incomes who live in our building, with priority for residents who use walkers or wheelchairs or who have fallen in the past year.

Many of our residents have lived here for more than a decade and want to stay among the friends and neighbors they know. Their families tell us that a fall is what they fear most. Making each apartment safer lets residents keep living independently in a home they already love.

## Outcomes

- Walk-in showers and grab bars installed in twenty apartments.
- Doorways widened in every apartment where a resident uses a wheelchair.
- Brighter lighting in all hallways and in upgraded apartments.
- Fewer falls at home among residents than in the year before.
- We will report the work completed and falls recorded to the Fund.

## Budget

- Walk-in showers with seats: $45,000
- Grab bars and handrails: $8,000
- Doorway widening: $14,000
- Lighting upgrades: $6,000
- Occupational therapist home assessments: $2,000

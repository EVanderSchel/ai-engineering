# First Homes: Transitional Housing for Women Leaving Shelter

## About us

Rosati Center exists to transform lives through sustainable housing, employment and healthcare, following the compassion of Jesus. We are a new organization and still very small: last year our total spending was $18, mostly on registration costs. Our founding volunteers have spent the past year planning our first program, meeting with shelters and landlords, and learning what women leaving shelter need most.

## The project

We request $5,000 to launch First Homes, a transitional housing program for women leaving emergency shelter. Shelter stays are short, and many women leave with nowhere stable to go. Without a deposit and first month's rent, they cannot sign a lease, even when they have found work. Many end up back in shelter, or in unsafe situations, within months.

First Homes will help women make that move. A volunteer mentor will work with each woman on a housing plan before she leaves shelter: finding an apartment she can afford, preparing a budget, and connecting with job training and healthcare. The grant will pay security deposits and essential household items, such as beds, kitchenware and linens, so that each woman can move into a home of her own. Mentors will meet with each woman every week for the first three months after she moves in, and every month for the rest of the year.

## Who it serves

In our first year we will help 3 to 4 women move from shelter into their own apartments. Each will be referred by a partner shelter and will have a source of income, through work or benefits, that can cover rent once she is settled.

## Outcomes

- Every woman served signs a lease and moves into her own home.
- Every woman is still housed six months after moving in.
- Every woman has a mentor and a written housing and work plan.
- We will report each move-in and six-month follow-up to the Fund.

## Budget

- Security deposits: $3,000
- Beds, kitchenware and household essentials: $1,500
- Mentor training: $500

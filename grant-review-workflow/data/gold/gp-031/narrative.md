# Summer Reading Camp for Children from Neighboring Public Schools

## About us

Charleston Day School fosters scholarship, integrity, respect and responsibility in our students, and partners with families to provide a strong academic, artistic, athletic and ethical foundation. Our annual operating budget is $6,614,000, and we have 94 paid staff, including teachers, reading specialists and support staff. Our reading specialists see every year how much ground children lose over the summer, and how much harder that loss is for children without books at home.

## The project

We request $24,000 to run a free five-week summer reading camp for rising second, third and fourth graders from neighboring public schools. Children from low-income families can lose two to three months of reading progress every summer, and the losses add up year after year. By fourth grade, the gap that began over the summers can be hard to close.

Campers will spend each morning on our campus in small reading groups led by our reading specialists and teachers, using the same structured methods we use with our own students. Afternoons will include library time, read-alouds, and activities that make reading fun: writing and illustrating their own books, drama, and visits from children's authors. Every child will take home ten books of their own choosing. Breakfast, lunch and bus transportation will be provided, so that cost and distance don't keep any child away.

## Who it serves

The camp will serve 40 children chosen by teachers at three neighboring public elementary schools, with priority for children reading below grade level and children from low-income families.

## Outcomes

- At least 35 of the 40 children attend at least four of the five weeks.
- Every child is assessed in reading at the start and end of camp.
- At least 80 percent of children keep or improve their reading level over the summer.
- We will report attendance and reading results to the Fund and to the children's schools in the fall.

## Budget

- Teacher and reading specialist stipends: $14,000
- Books for children to keep: $4,000
- Bus transportation: $3,500
- Classroom materials and author visits: $2,500

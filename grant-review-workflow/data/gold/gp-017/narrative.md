# Summer Jobs for Teens in Downtown Paducah

## About us

Beautiful Paducah is committed to the beautification and preservation of Paducah, Kentucky, with a focus on the downtown and historic districts. We host festivals, events and community programs that bring people downtown and support the local economy. Our annual operating budget is $300,000. Our events depend on young people to set up, clean up and keep downtown looking its best, and we have seen how much a first job can mean to a teenager who has never had one.

## The project

We request $27,500 to create a summer jobs program for teenagers from low-income families. For many teens, a first paid job is the start of everything that follows: a reference, a work history, and the habits of showing up on time and working with others. Teens from families without connections are the least likely to find that first job on their own.

Each summer, 15 teens will be hired for eight weeks to maintain downtown planters, parks and historic sites, help run community events, and assist local businesses. Every teen will be paired with an adult supervisor and will spend one afternoon a week in job-readiness workshops covering resumes, interviews, money management and workplace skills. At the end of the summer, every teen will leave with a reference letter and help applying for a school-year job.

## Who it serves

The program will serve 15 teens aged 15 to 18 each summer, referred by school counselors and youth programs, with priority for teens from low-income households and teens who have never held a job.

## Outcomes

- At least 13 of the 15 teens complete the full eight weeks.
- Every teen completes the job-readiness workshops and leaves with a resume.
- At least 10 teens find a school-year job or internship by October.
- We will report hiring, completion and job placement to the Fund at the end of the summer.

## Budget

- Teen wages: $21,000
- Supervisor time: $3,500
- Job-readiness workshops: $1,500
- Work gear and safety equipment: $1,500

# Renovating Our Union Hall

## About us

The Iron Workers Trust Fund Local No. 5 Washington DC Welfare Fund provides health benefits to union employees covered by a collective bargaining agreement. Our annual operating budget is $19,629,000. Our members and their families rely on the fund for their health coverage, and our trustees and members meet regularly to oversee it.

## The project

We request $10,000 to renovate the meeting hall and clubhouse where our trustees, members and retirees gather. The hall has not been updated in many years. The carpet is worn, the kitchen equipment is old, the lighting is poor, and the restrooms need work. Members use the hall for trustee meetings, benefit information sessions, retirement parties and the annual holiday gathering, and it no longer reflects the pride our members take in their union.

The renovation will replace the flooring in the main room, update the kitchen with new appliances, install brighter lighting, and refresh the restrooms and paint throughout. Union members in the building trades have offered to volunteer some of the labor, which keeps the cost down. Work will be scheduled between meetings so that the hall stays available for regular events, and we expect it to be finished within three months.

## Who it serves

The hall is used by the fund's trustees, staff, members and retirees, and by their families at social events throughout the year. A renovated hall will make these gatherings more comfortable and welcoming for everyone who uses it.

## Outcomes

- New flooring, kitchen appliances and lighting installed.
- Restrooms and walls refreshed throughout.
- The renovation finished within three months, with no cancelled meetings.
- We will send the Fund photos of the finished hall and a summary of the work and costs.

## Budget

- Flooring: $4,000
- Kitchen appliances: $3,000
- Lighting: $1,500
- Paint and restroom updates: $1,500

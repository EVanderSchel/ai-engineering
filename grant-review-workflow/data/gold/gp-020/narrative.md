# Trauma Therapy for Children After Abuse

## About us

The Lake County Children's Advocacy Center coordinates the response to allegations of child sexual and physical abuse in Lake County. A multi-disciplinary team of prosecutors, mental health staff from the county health department, victim advocates, child welfare investigators, medical providers, law enforcement and forensic interviewers works together so that each child is interviewed once, in a child-friendly setting, and gets the help they need. Our annual operating budget is $154,000.

## The project

We request $31,000 to provide trauma-focused therapy to children seen at our center. Children who have been abused are at high risk of depression, anxiety, post-traumatic stress and problems in school, and treatment works best when it starts soon after disclosure. Today, many families leave our center with a referral but wait months for a therapist who is trained in treating child trauma, and some never begin treatment at all.

The grant will pay licensed therapists trained in evidence-based trauma treatment for children to see children at our center, in the same safe and familiar place where they were interviewed. Treatment usually lasts 12 to 20 weekly sessions and includes the child's non-offending parent or caregiver, who learns how to support the child at home. Sessions will be free, and evening appointments will be offered for working parents. Our advocates will help families with transportation so that distance doesn't keep a child from finishing treatment.

## Who it serves

We expect about 35 children between the ages of 4 and 17 to begin therapy over the year, along with their caregivers. All will be children seen at our center after an allegation of abuse.

## Outcomes

- Every child referred begins therapy within two weeks.
- At least 75 percent of children who begin treatment complete it.
- Children's trauma symptom scores fall between the start and end of treatment.
- We will report children served, sessions completed and symptom scores to the Fund twice a year.

## Budget

- Licensed trauma therapists: $26,000
- Therapy materials and assessments: $2,000
- Transportation help for families: $3,000

# Respite Care for Family Caregivers

## About us

Southeast Missouri Area Agency on Aging develops a coordinated system of services that helps older Missourians remain in their homes and enhances their quality of life. Our annual operating budget is $10,541,000, and we have 63 paid staff, including care coordinators and case managers who work with older adults and their families every day. More and more of the families we work with are caring for a parent or spouse at home, and the caregivers are wearing out.

## The project

We request $5,000 to expand respite care for family caregivers of older adults. Caregivers who never get a break are more likely to become ill, depressed or injured themselves, and when a caregiver collapses, the person they care for often ends up in a nursing home. A few hours away, on a regular schedule, can be what keeps a family caring at home.

The grant will fund in-home respite: a trained aide who comes to the home for a few hours each week so that the caregiver can rest, run errands, see a doctor or simply have time to themselves. Our care coordinators will identify the caregivers under the most strain, using a short caregiver stress assessment, and will check in with each family every month. Caregivers will also be invited to a monthly support group, with respite provided so they can attend.

## Who it serves

The grant will provide regular respite to 8 to 10 family caregivers over the year, most of them spouses or adult children caring for someone with dementia or a serious chronic illness. Priority goes to caregivers with the highest stress scores and those with no other help.

## Outcomes

- Every family served receives respite at least twice a month.
- Caregivers' stress scores improve between the first and last assessment.
- Every person cared for by a participating caregiver is still living at home at the end of the year.
- We will report families served, respite hours and stress scores to the Fund.

## Budget

- In-home respite aide hours: $4,200
- Caregiver support group costs: $500
- Caregiver stress assessments and materials: $300
